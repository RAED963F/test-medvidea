Procedures within plastic surgery

Damas Medical Center covers the full spectrum of aesthetic and reconstructive work.

Rhinoplasty

Reshaping and functional correction of the nose, balancing profile and breathing.

Face & Eyelids

Facelift, blepharoplasty and rejuvenation for a natural, well-rested look.

Breast Surgery

Augmentation, reduction, lift and reconstruction, tailored to each patient.

Body Contouring

Liposuction, abdominoplasty and post–weight-loss contouring of the figure.

Reconstructive Surgery

Restoring form and function after trauma, surgery or congenital differences.

Hair Transplantation

Modern follicular techniques for natural, lasting hair restoration.
